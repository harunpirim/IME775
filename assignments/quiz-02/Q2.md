### Question 3

**(a)**

$$\vec{w}^{(1)} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ -3 \end{bmatrix} - 0.1 \begin{bmatrix} 10 \\ -12 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ -1.8 \end{bmatrix}$$

**(b)**

$$L(\vec{w}^{(0)}) = 5(1)^2 + 2(-3)^2 = 5 + 18 = 23$$
$$L(\vec{w}^{(1)}) = 5(0)^2 + 2(-1.8)^2 = 0 + 6.48 = 6.48$$

Yes, the loss decreased from 23 to 6.48.

### Question 5

**(a)** $f(x) = x^2$, $f'(x) = 2x$, $f(3) = 9$, $f'(3) = 6$.

$$f(3.2) \approx 9 + 0.2 \times 6 = 9 + 1.2 = \boxed{10.2}$$

**(b)** Exact: $f(3.2) = 10.24$. Error $= |10.24 - 10.2| = 0.04$.
