Key Insight

L1 gradient = ±1 (constant magnitude)

Pushes weights toward zero with the same force regardless of magnitude. A weight at w = 0.001 gets the same push as w = 100. Weights reach exactly zero.

L2 gradient = 2w (proportional to w)

Push weakens as w → 0. Weights shrink but never reach zero. This produces dense vectors with small values.

Bayesian view: L2 ↔ Gaussian prior, L1 ↔ Laplace prior (sharp peak at zero).
