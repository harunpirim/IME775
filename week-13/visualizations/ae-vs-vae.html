What you are looking at. A synthetic 10-class 2D latent-space simulation. The autoencoder panel shows codes that minimize reconstruction with no regularization — clusters stay separable but spread widely, with large empty regions that decode to garbage. The VAE panel shows the same clusters pulled toward the origin by the KL term to N(0, I) (the orange dashed circle marks the unit ball). Raise β to see clusters shrink and overlap; lower β to see the VAE behave more like the AE.
