Why the trick matters. Neural networks learn by backpropagating gradients. A direct call like z = torch.normal(mu, sigma) is a non-differentiable stochastic node — gradients cannot flow through the sampling step. By rewriting sampling as z = μ + σ · ε with ε drawn from a fixed distribution, the randomness is detached from the computational graph and gradients w.r.t. μ and σ can be computed analytically. The encoder can then be trained end-to-end by ordinary backpropagation.
