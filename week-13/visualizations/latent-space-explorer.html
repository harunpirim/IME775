What you are seeing. A 2D latent space where each class occupies a Gaussian cluster around some learned mean. Moving the cursor selects any z in the plane; the right panel decodes that z by blending class prototypes weighted by closeness to each cluster centre — a stand-in for what a real VAE decoder neural network does. Traverse between two clusters to see smooth morphing — this is why VAE latent spaces are useful for generation and interpolation. Drifting far from the clusters produces blurry / mixed outputs — the latent region there was never trained on.
