KL(q ‖ p) = ½ ( μ² + σ² − 2 ln σ − 1 )
Why this matters. In a VAE, the encoder outputs μ and log σ² for every input. The KL term pushes those outputs toward (0, 1): the latent distribution concentrates near the origin with unit variance, giving a compact, continuous latent space that we can sample from at inference time. Try μ = 0, σ = 1 → KL = 0.
